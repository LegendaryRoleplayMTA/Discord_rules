🛡️ BASIC DISCORD RULES

Thamizhan Roleplay Community Guidelines

*** Please read and follow all the rules carefully to keep this server safe and fun for everyone.***

1. RESPECT EVERYONE

Respect everyone in our server. Don't speak bad words or inappropriate words. Don't shout in voice channels.

• No toxic behavior, hate speech, or bullying is allowed.

2. NO BLANK OR INAPPROPRIATE NICKNAMES

Don't use any weird or inappropriate name is strictly prohibited.

• Don’t use empty, offensive, or adult nicknames.

3. USE A PROPER PROFILE PIC

Don't use any weird or inappropriate profile picture is strictly prohibited.

• Your profile picture should not be empty, NSFW, or inappropriate in any way.

4. STAFF DISCRETION

Follow all staff instructions and general server policies.

• Staff have the right to take action, including changing names or applying rules, even if not clearly written here.

5. NO MASS PING

Don't spam tags to call someone.

• Don’t tag many people or roles without a good reason or permission.

6. NO EXPLOITING RULES

Follow the rules properly without trying to circumvent them.

• Don’t try to cheat or find ways around the rules. If you do, strict action will be taken by the staff, which may include warnings, kicks, temporary bans, or even permanent bans depending on the seriousness of the violation. Always follow the rules with the right intention.

7. NO DM PROMOTION

No DM promotion in our server (it leads to permanent ban). Raise a ticket if anyone sends DM promotions to you.

• Don’t advertise other servers, products, or services through direct messages.

8. NO SPAMMING

Don't send any other server links or inappropriate links in our server.

• Don’t send repeated messages, random links, or too many caps/emojis. You may be muted, kicked, or banned depending on how serious it is.

🎙️ Voice & IC/OOC Guidelines

  • Don't speak about other servers in our TRP server voice channel.
  • Don't talk about IC scenario problems in voice channels or OOC chat (just raise a ticket).
  • Don't share your personal information with others.

🏆 THAMIZHAN ROLEPLAY

🛡️ Keep the community safe. 🤝 Respect other players. ⚖️ Follow the rules.
